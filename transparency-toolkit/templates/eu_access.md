# Access to EU Documents Request Template

Use this outline when requesting records under Regulation 1049/2001 in the
European Union. Address the request to the EMA or relevant national competent
authority. Reference Article 2 access rights and provide an overriding public
interest justification for disclosure.

Request data in open formats and specify that the information will be used for
independent, peer-reviewed scientific work aimed at assessing vaccine safety and
surveillance system performance.
