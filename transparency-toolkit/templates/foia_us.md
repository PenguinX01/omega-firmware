RE: Freedom of Information Act Request – V-Safe Complete Dataset

Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. § 552, I hereby request the following records:

REQUESTED RECORDS:
Complete, de-identified V-Safe survey dataset from December 14, 2020 to present, including:

• Unique participant identifier (randomized)
• Vaccination details: date, dose sequence, product name, lot number
• All symptom reports: codes, severity ratings, free-text entries
• Healthcare utilization flags: clinic visits, emergency department, hospitalization
• Demographics: age band, sex, geographic region (state/county FIPS)
• Survey completion patterns and follow-up duration

FORMAT: CSV, JSON, or Parquet with complete data dictionary

JUSTIFICATION:
This request serves the public interest by enabling independent scientific analysis of vaccine safety signals. The data will support peer-reviewed research on surveillance system effectiveness and long-term safety monitoring.

FEE WAIVER REQUEST:
I request a complete fee waiver under 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) as:
1. This request is for non-commercial scientific research
2. Public release will contribute significantly to public understanding
3. I am not affiliated with commercial interests

If any portions are withheld, please provide a detailed Vaughn index specifying the legal basis for each exemption.

EXPEDITED PROCESSING REQUESTED:
This information is urgently needed for ongoing scientific research with public health implications.

Sincerely,
