About the King's Chamber

The King's Chamber in the Great Pyramid of Giza is renowned for its unique acoustic properties. Many researchers have reported a fundamental resonance around 117 Hz, which corresponds to the F# note. This simulator allows you to explore how different parameters affect the chamber's resonant frequencies.
