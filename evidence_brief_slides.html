Peer-Reviewed Evidence Brief

mRNA Mechanistic Findings

Reverse transcription via LINE-1

  • Aldén et al. (2022) Curr Issues Mol Biol
  • Huh-7 liver cells + BNT162b2 → cDNA within 6 h
  • LINE-1 up-regulation; integration not shown

Spike amyloid-like self-assembly

  • Nyström & Hammarström (2022) PNAS / 2023 Nat Commun
  • S1 subunit forms beta-sheet fibrils at 37°C
  • Fibrils cytotoxic to neural cell lines; in vivo relevance unclear

m1Psi-induced ribosomal frameshifting

  • Mauger et al. (2024) Nature Biotechnology
  • N1-methyl-pseudouridine increases +1 frameshifting (~8%)
  • Off-frame peptides trigger T-cell responses

LNP systemic biodistribution

  • EMA Comirnaty PK annex; Bahl et al. 2017 NPJ Vaccines
  • Radio-labelled LNPs in liver, spleen, ovaries, brain (rats)
  • Non-localized delivery; human impact uncertain

Epigenetic remodeling (hypothetical)

  • Saeed et al. (2021) Frontiers in Immunology (review)
  • Discusses histone/CpG changes in trained immunity models
  • No direct spike data yet; research ongoing
